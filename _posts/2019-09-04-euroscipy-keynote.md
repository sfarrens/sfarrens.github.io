In September 2019 I had the pleasure of giving a keynote talk at [EuroScipy 2019](https://www.euroscipy.org/2019/) in Bilbao, Spain. The talk, titled **"From Galaxies to Brains! — Image processing with Python"**, gave an overview of how tools developed for astrophysics can be applied to problems in medical imaging, and vice versa.

<!--more-->

The talk covers weak gravitational lensing, PSF modelling, sparse image reconstruction, and the [PySAP](https://github.com/CEA-COSMIC/pysap) software package, which brings together methods from astrophysics and MRI.
